What this proves

      
The wallet gives Budborne a public address only after you approve the connection. The proof button asks the wallet to sign a plain-text challenge. Budborne verifies that signature in your browser. No token moves, no transaction is sent, and nothing touches mainnet.

      
Never type a seed phrase or private key into Budborne. A real wallet connection should never ask for either.
